Expected Impact

The proposed solution aims to reduce manual inspection effort, improve consistency in compliance checks, and support consumer protection and fair trade practices.

Key Capabilities & Features

  • Extract text from product labels using OCR.
  • Identify manufacturer or packer details.
  • Check net quantity and maximum retail price (MRP).
  • Extract manufacturing, packing, or import date information.
  • Identify consumer care details and other required declarations.
  • Validate extracted information against configurable compliance rules.
  • Flag potential violations and generate understandable compliance reports.

SIH 2026 Detailed Brief

Automated Packaged Commodity Compliance Checker

Problem Statement ID: 26034 | Category: Compliance Automation

Regulatory Background

Under the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 and the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011, every pre-packaged commodity sold across physical retail and e-commerce portals must carry mandatory declarations. These include the genuine manufacturer/packer identity, net quantity (verified with correct units of measurement), Maximum Retail Price (inclusive of all taxes), dates of manufacture/pack, and consumer grievance contact details.

The Challenge

Regulatory authorities and online platforms currently face manual inspection bottlenecks. With millions of SKUs and variable label orientations, manual checks result in delayed reviews and inconsistencies in consumer rights protection.

Step-by-Step Technical Architecture

Step 1
Image Acquisition & Normalization: Input label photos or e-commerce listing images are pre-processed (deskewing, contrast enhancement, denoising).
Step 2
Optical Character Recognition (OCR): Segmented packaging regions are scanned to convert text pixels into searchable Unicode strings.
Step 3
Entity & Field Extraction: Heuristic and regular expression parsers isolate MRP, Net Weight/Volume, Batch numbers, and Customer Care emails/phone numbers.
Step 4
Configurable Rule Engine: Extracted fields are evaluated against Legal Metrology Rules (e.g. font size thresholds, mandatory units, non-deceptive representation).
Step 5
Automated Audit Report: The system flags missing or non-compliant fields with confidence highlights and generates an exportable inspection report.

Planned Implementation Stack

Python Tesseract OCR / EasyOCR OpenCV (Image Preprocessing) Regular Expressions & Parsers JSON / PDF Report Exporter

Current Milestone

Project Status: Proposed & Architectural Planning.
